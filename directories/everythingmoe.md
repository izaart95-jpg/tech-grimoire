# EverythingMoe — The Otaku Site Directory

This document covers EverythingMoe, a curated toplist/index for all of Otaku culture. It is **not itself a streamer or a reader** — it is a hand-reviewed directory that ranks roughly a thousand anime/manga/Otaku-related sites and apps, making it the single best discovery hub for finding working sites in any Otaku category (anime streaming, manga/manhwa readers, donghua, light novels, J-drama/Webtoons ecosystem, OST/music, apps/tools and more). All facts below were checked against the live site in October 2026.

> **Note:** EverythingMoe indexes and reviews third-party sites, including gray-area aggregators. Its own About page states clearly: *"We review websites, not recommend them."* Verify the legal status of anything you use, and always run an ad blocker — sites listed there often serve aggressive ads (EverythingMoe itself says so and tells users to install one).

## 1. What It Is

- **Website:** https://www.everythingmoe.com
- **Type:** Curated directory/toplist — an index of anime & Otaku-culture sites, not a streaming platform
- **Tagline:** "Curated toplist of everything Otaku culture"
- **Status clock:** homepage shows "Last update: X hours ago" — the list is re-ranked continuously

### Overview (from their About page)

EverythingMoe lists and reviews sites/apps related to Anime/Manga (Otaku) culture, with each review manually crafted from the team's own testing (not automated). The goal is an up-to-date, reliable list of the best Anime/Manga websites for an English-speaking audience. They define "Otaku culture" broadly as East-Asian entertainment subcultures — effectively ACG (anime, comics, games) culture — which is why the index is far wider than just anime streaming.

Key mechanics (all stated on their About/Info page):

- **Community project:** independent, explicitly "does NOT aim to make money", does not accept donations; contributions via their forum (`/forum/?board=suggest`) or Discord
- **Site requirements:** category-appropriate, accessible (not down/restricted/broken), enough content, not deceptive or malicious toward users, usable by English speakers
- **Rank criteria:** popularity + track record and content-library size are "very important"; reliability, content quality, UI/usability are "important"; features and community interaction are "less important"; **ad cleanliness is only a "less important" factor** — sites with bad ads are still listed, just with a negative point
- **Grouped entries:** one entry can contain multiple links, grouped when sites share identical infrastructure, belong to the same site (alternate domains), or are near-duplicates
- **Graveyard:** dead/retired sites are moved to the **Graveyard** rather than silently dropped (the homepage literally shows notices like "AnimeKai is moved to Graveyard"); the About page adds that a site is only excluded from review if it fails the requirements or would be too high-maintenance
- **Community channels:** r/EverythingMoe, r/StreamingAnime (Reddit), Bluesky (@everythingmoe), Discord, Matrix

### What It Is Not

- It is **not a streamer** — despite its name appearing on streamer lists, everythingmoe.com hosts no video
- It is not a tracker, downloader, or database — it links to such sites but hosts none of them

**Filters** on each section refine entries by trait chips. Verified on the live site:

```
Anime:   Self-host | Scraper | Hard-sub | Soft-sub | Dub friendly | Easy download | Modern interface
Manga:   Official Rips | Licensed | Scanlator | Hub | Mostly Scans | Multi Language | Raws | Modern interface
```

**Tag markers** distinguish special classes of entry (also used on the unofficial ecosystem tools):

- `HUB` — aggregates multiple sites (e.g., Comix, Mangadotnet, MangaFire)
- `MULT` — multi-link/grouped entry (alternate domains, near-duplicates)
- `DDL` — direct download (e.g., Anna's Archive)
- `RAW` / `LICENSED` / `SCAN` / `TORRENT` / `NSFW` — content-type tags in the site's data
- `NEW` — recently added
- `KOTO` — marks clone/reupload sites that run on **Anikoto's** player/API (e.g., Aniverse, Enma, aniwavecomse — the site notes for each: "use Anikoto stream", "Content depends on Anikoto", "not original"). See the Anikoto note in section 3.

## 3. Anime Streaming Sites (Oct 2026)

These sit at the top of EverythingMoe's Anime Streaming section as of October 2026 (ranks shift between checks as the list re-ranks):

- **Re:Anime** — reanime.to — ranked #1 — **verified alive (HTTP 200), Oct 2026**
- **Anikoto** — anikototv.to — ranked #2 — **verified alive (HTTP 200)**. Caution: the site's own review notes it "Reupload HiAnime videos", has "Bad ads", *"It also has many alt domain and brand names. It usually copies other websites."* Entries tagged `KOTO` on the list are clones/reuploads riding Anikoto's player and API.
- **animepahe** — animepahe.pw (alternates .com/.org/.pw listed by EverythingMoe) — ranked #3 — **server responds in Oct 2026 (403 to scripted requests — it blocks bots, not browsers)**. Known for hand-picked video sources, low data usage, and a long track record; dubs exist but are not its priority.
- **Miruro** — miruro.to — ranked #4 — **verified alive (HTTP 200), Oct 2026**. Keeps many mirrors/backups: miruro.cx, miruro.bz, miruro.tv, miruro.com, anivault.tv (listed on EverythingMoe's entry). "animeyee" and "animeuwutv" are forks by other people, not the original.
- **AniZone** — anizone.to — ranked ~#6 — **verified alive (HTTP 200), Oct 2026**. Self-hosted server, rich subtitle typesetting, medium-small library, great for recent/airing anime (older titles hard-sub, newer soft-sub).
- **Senshi** — ninstream.com (domain from EverythingMoe's data) — ranked ~#7 — responds to custom-User-Agent requests; use the entry page for its current domain.

The legit tier is listed alongside: **Crunchyroll, Netflix, Hulu, Bilibili, YouTube, HIDIVE, RetroCrush**.

### Context for October 2026

- **HiAnime (hianime.to, formerly Zoro.to → Aniwatch.to) is dead.** It went offline 13 March 2026 with a goodbye message; staff confirmed permanent closure on 1 June 2026; on 2 July 2026 the Alliance for Creativity and Entertainment (ACE) confirmed Vietnam's Ministry of Public Security arrested seven operators (charges include copyright infringement and money laundering; prosecutors cited 100+ sites, ~26,000 titles, ~$12.85M in ad revenue 2020–Apr 2026). HiAnime had peaked at 364M visits/month (Oct 2024) — more than Crunchyroll — and was on the USTR Notorious Markets list.
- **Every "HiAnime" site still online today (hianimez.org, hianime.help, *.ms, *.se, etc.) is an unverified clone or impostor** — several are listed not as original sites but in EverythingMoe's Anikoto-family entries. Treat any hianime* domain as untrusted.
- **AnimeKai** ceased operations May 2026 and is marked on the homepage: "AnimeKai is moved to Graveyard".

## 5. Site Rotation Caveat

Free streaming/aggregator sites rotate domains constantly — operators move when a domain is blocked or seized, and expired domains are frequently re-registered by copycats and malware farms. Consequences:

1. **Any specific URL in this file can be stale** within weeks. The ranked entry links on everythingmoe.com are kept current ("Last update: hours ago" at all times).
2. **Name ≠ site.** After a takedown (e.g., HiAnime), clones using the exact same name proliferate. EverythingMoe's `KOTO` flags and grouped-clone entries (Anikoto-clones, AniWave-clones, HiAnime-name sites in the Anikoto entry) exist precisely to keep the impostors documented rather than misranked.
3. Landing pages imitating popular sites (often SEO-optimized, hosted on random TLDs) are common. Cross-check any domain against EverythingMoe before using it.

## 6. Using the Toplist, Filters, Graveyard & Detector

Workflow for finding a working site for any Otaku need:

```
1. Go to https://www.everythingmoe.com
2. Pick your section (Anime, Donghua, Manga, Manhwa, Novel, Drama,
   Games, Apps, Download, Music, ...)
3. Apply filters (e.g., "Dub friendly", "Easy download", "Multi Language")
4. Pick a high-ranked entry — skip entries flagged KOTO
5. Open the entry page (everythingmoe.com/s/<name>) to read the review:
   pros/cons, notes, grouped mirrors/altlink domains
6. Bookmark the ENTRY page, not just the raw domain —
   if the domain rotates, the entry page links the current one
7. Follow r/EverythingMoe for changelog news about takedowns and new domains
```

Support pages verified live (links in the site header):

- **Main Index** — the ranked lists
- **Graveyard** (`/graveyard`) — officially dead sites; check here to confirm a site is really dead before hunting for "new domain" claims on random blogs (those are often the impostors themselves)
- **Detector** (`/monitor`) — the site's Downtime Detector: per-site online/offline status and latency so you can tell whether a site is down for everyone or just blocked by your ISP
- **Activity** (`/activity.html`) — change feed of new entries and rank movements

Tips:

- **Use an ad blocker** on any site you discover through it (uBlock Origin). EverythingMoe itself is clean, but many listed sites are not — the project itself says bad-ads sites are listed regardless and reminds users to install one.
- **Cross-check** the "Last update" clock on the homepage — it refreshes every few hours, so a fresh timestamp means the list has been actively maintained.

## 7. Programmatic Verification

The list is scriptable for link audits: the ranked lists are server-rendered in the homepage HTML, and per-entry data (pros/cons, notes, grouped mirrors, tag chips) is published as JSON at https://everythingmoe.com/data/cache/main.json. An unofficial MIT-licensed viewer (github.com/quantavil/everythingmoe) exposes that dataset with an on-demand liveness checker.

```bash
# Quick liveness check of a batch of candidate domains (works from the dataset too)
for d in reanime.to anikototv.to animepahe.pw miruro.to anizone.to \
         weebcentral.com mangadex.org mihon.app; do
  code=$(curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}" -L --max-time 12 \
    -A "Mozilla/5.0" "https://$d/")
  echo "$d -> $code"
done
```

Anything returning 403 (animepahe does this to scripts) is typically bot-blocking, not death — open it in a browser or check its EverythingMoe entry / the Detector page.
